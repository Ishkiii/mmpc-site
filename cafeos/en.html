From signing in a player to closing the shift. No paper, no spreadsheets, no guessing who played for how long.

Price per hour, walk-in guests, members with prepaid balance and VIP discounts. Add time with one click.

Point of sale and tables

Sell drinks and snacks, run a table bill paid together or per person, and take orders players send from their PC.

Reservations

Book a specific PC or any free one. At the booked time the session starts by itself and billing begins.

See any PC's screen, or take over its mouse and keyboard from the counter. Restart, shut down and maintenance in one click.

Shifts and reports

Members and points

Member accounts with deposits, points and history. New members get a one-time code and choose their own password.

Automatic updates

Free PCs on your website

No new hardware. CafeOS runs on the ordinary Windows PCs you already have.

  1. You get the installer

    We send you a single setup file prepared for your cafe.

    0 min on your side

  2. Install it on the counter PC

    Double-click, then a few clicks in the wizard: cafe name, currency and price per hour.

    about 5 min

  3. Connect the gaming PCs

// faq

Frequently asked questions

Does CafeOS work without internet?

Yes. Everything runs on the PC in your cafe over the local network. Internet is only needed for updates, renewing the subscription and remote support.

Do I need new hardware?

No. You need one Windows 10 or 11 PC as the server (it can be the counter PC) and the gaming PCs you already have.

How long does setup take?

Installing on the server takes about 5 minutes, and connecting each gaming PC about 10 minutes. We help you remotely the whole time.

Can players buy time or order drinks themselves?

Yes, from their own PC. If you'd rather they didn't, turn it off in Settings and sell everything at the counter.

What if an update fails?

CafeOS makes a backup before updating and puts the previous version back by itself if anything goes wrong. It only updates when nobody is playing.

Where is my data?

On the PC in your cafe, with an automatic daily backup. You can also make a backup by hand with one click.

How much does it cost after the trial?

It depends on the number of PCs. Send a request below or message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you an offer.
